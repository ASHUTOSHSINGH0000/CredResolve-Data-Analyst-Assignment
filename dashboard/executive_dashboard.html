Data quality flags

  • 98.46% of calls have borrower IDs that disagree with the account borrower ID.
  • 92.85% of PROMISE_BROKEN targets do not have a prior broken PTP event.
  • About 66.7% of calls have a timezone mismatch with the account.
  • Borrower-level analysis is therefore treated as unreliable.

₹10 Cr recommendation

Better borrower targeting, but use a controlled pilot first.

There is a clear targeting-quality problem, while the data does not give a strong causal case for more agents, AI voice, digital, field or telephony.

A full ₹10 Cr rollout is not justified from this observational dataset alone. Test the uplift first and add cost data.
